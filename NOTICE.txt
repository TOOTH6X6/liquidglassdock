LiquidGlassDock
This project includes a Metal port of the refraction, rounded-rectangle SDF and rim-highlight
algorithms from "Backdrop" (AndroidLiquidGlass-kmp) by Kyant,
Copyright 2025 Kyant, licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (see LICENSE).

Changes: rewritten from AGSL/Kotlin to Metal Shading Language and Objective-C; corner radius
is selected from the centred coordinate; chromatic aberration reduced to 3 samples; blur is a
one-time MPS Gaussian blur; added screen capture and SpringBoard Dock hook.
